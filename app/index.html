Picture reading is approximate. Crop the screenshot to just the candles (no price labels, icons or tags), red and green candles on a dark background. Hover over the highest and lowest wick on your chart to read their prices, and over the last candle for its time. Fill in the four boxes first, then choose the file. Each price can be off by a few ticks, and the picture holds only a screen of candles, so the backtester will not have enough history to approve trades. Use it to see what the scanner flags; for real testing load a CSV.
